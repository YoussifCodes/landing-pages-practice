# Product Requirements

## Product Name

**Vyra One** — a flagship smartphone (placeholder brand name, replace freely).
Landing page title: "Vyra One — Built to Move."

## Vision

Make the Vyra One feel like the most desirable phone of the year before anyone reads a single spec. The landing page should deliver a cinematic, scroll-driven first impression that turns curiosity into a pre-order.

## Target Audience

- Primary: ages 18–35, style-conscious, mobile-first users who care about camera quality, design, and battery life.
- Secondary: content creators and gamers who need performance and a strong camera.
- Behavior: arrive from social ads and short videos on a phone, scan quickly, decide on visuals and price, compare against 2–3 competitors.

## Problem Statement

- Most phone landing pages are spec dumps: long tables, generic stock renders, no emotion.
- Visitors can't tell within seconds why this phone is different or worth the price.
- Heavy pages load slowly on mobile, which is where most of the traffic comes from.

## Proposed Solution

A single-page, scroll-driven landing page where the phone is the hero. Each scroll step reveals one benefit (camera, display, battery, performance, design) with large type, glass cards, and motion. Specs are shown as short, scannable stats instead of tables. A persistent CTA leads to pre-order.

## Business Goal

- Drive pre-orders and "Notify me" sign-ups for the launch.
- Build an email list before launch day.
- Reinforce brand positioning as premium but approachable.

## User Goals

- Understand in under 10 seconds what the phone is and why it's special.
- See the design, colors, and camera results clearly.
- Check key specs and price quickly.
- Choose a color/storage option and order, or save the product for later.
- Trust the purchase (warranty, delivery, return policy).

## Core Features

1. Cinematic hero with the phone, bold headline, and primary CTA.
2. Scroll-driven feature chapters: Camera, Display, Battery, Performance, Design.
3. Color picker that swaps the phone image and accent color live.
4. Glassmorphism stat cards (e.g., 120Hz, 5000mAh, 50MP).
5. Compact specs section (expandable) instead of a long table.
6. Pricing and storage options with a pre-order button.
7. Notify-me email capture.
8. FAQ and trust badges (warranty, shipping, returns).
9. Sticky header with CTA that persists while scrolling.
10. Fully responsive layout, light on mobile bandwidth.

## Success Metrics

- Pre-order / "Notify me" conversion rate: target ≥ 4%.
- Hero CTA click-through rate: ≥ 12%.
- Average scroll depth ≥ 65%.
- Mobile Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 s.
- Bounce rate under 45%.
- Email sign-ups collected before launch day (set a numeric goal per campaign).
